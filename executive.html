Make your experience easy to describe

At senior level, the next role often comes through someone who recommends you. That only works if they can say in a sentence what you're known for.

Executive is the last step, a small group built on Kate's Personal Brand course. You complete the worksheet in the session, read it back to the group, and turn it into the words for your headline, About section and bio.

Questions

Who can book Executive?

People who've completed Fundamentals and Advanced. It's built for director level and above, with 15 or more years of experience.

Why only six people?

Everyone reads their brand statement back and gets feedback. A small group leaves time for each person.

Do I get the recording?

Yes. Everyone who books gets the recording afterward.

Where does it happen?

Live on Zoom. You'll get the link before the session.

What if I can't make my date?

We'll move your seat to next month's date. Refunds are available up to 24 hours before the workshop, as long as the workbook hasn't been sent.

What if only a few people book?

Each workshop needs three people to run. If a date has fewer, it moves to the next month and your seat moves with it.
